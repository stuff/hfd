TO SAVE THE RARE MEMORY !!

USING 16 MHZ WHILE DECRUNCHING !

CREATE THAT SUPRA-FAST HIFI-

PAULA WHICH RUNS ON ST AT 16 KHZ

THAT STE-OWNERS HAVE GOT BETTER

QUALITY THAN ON AMIGA !!!

REALTIME VOLUME- AND FREQUENCY-

VARIATION INCLUDED OF COZ !!

IT TAKES ABOUT 60 PERCENT !!

---- SHINING 8 PARTY ----
PAGE -1-

IN APRIL SOME MEMBERS OF AURA

(AEON.CHRIS AND CLASH) MADE A

TRIP TO THE EASTERN CONFERENCE

92 OF THE AMIGA CREW SHINING 8.

ACTUALLY THE PARTY WAS HELD THE

SAME TIME WHILE THE MEGA LEIF

CONVENTION IN SWEDEN! IN FACT WE

WENT TO THE AMIGA PARTY COZ IT

WAS HELD IN -LAHR- (A TOWN NEAR

THE AURA HEADQUARTER -OFFENBURG-

ABOUT 30 KILOMETRES DISTANCE)

THE PARTY TOOK PLACE IN A ....

PAGE -2-

DISCO CALLED -TERRAS-! (A SMALL

DISCO INCLUDING A BISTRO) WE GOT

THERE BY CAR: CHRIS.HIS BROTHER

AND CLASH WITH AN OLD VW KAEFER.

AEON AND MR.X OF D-TECT WITH A

BRANDNEW FIAT UNO. THE PARTY

STARTED AT 2PM! WE ARRIVED

ABOUT 3PM AND FOUND ABOUT 100

PEOPLE SITTING IN FRONT OF THEIR

AMIGAS WHILE COPYING ....

COMPOSING.... PAINTING ....

CODING.... GAMBLING ....

PAGE -3-

WE FOUND A FREE TABLE AND BUILT

UP OUR AMIGA.... SOME GUYS BEGAN

TO COPY THE LATEST DEMOS WHILE

AEON AND CHRIS WALKED AROUND TO

SEE WHAT AMIGA VIPS WERE THERE

AT THE MEETING.... WE MET SOME

INTERESTING GUYS FROM SANITY

(ZAP AND ANOTHER CODER)! THEY

RELEASED THEIR NEW SOUNDDISK

CALLED -JESTERDAY- AT THIS PARTY

WITH SONGS FROM JESTER. WE

REALLY MADE NICE TALKS....

PAGE -4-

ABOUT 6PM WE MADE A JOURNEY

TO MC DONALDS... AFTER SOME

BURGERS.. CHICKENS AND COKES

WE RETURNED TO -TERRAS- ! THE

INVITATORS ANNOUNCED A SOUND

GFX AND DEMO-COMPETITION AND

AT 9PM THE SOUND-COMPETITON

WAS EXPECTED BUT ACTUALLY

IT BEGAN AT MIDNIGHT! ABOUT

10PM ALL PEOPLE WALKED TO

THE DANCE FLOOR TO SEE THE

SHOW OF THE BELLY DANCER!

PAGE -5-

ACTUALLY THE INVITATORS

ANNOUNCED A LIVE STRIP.TOO.BUT

IT SHOULD BE ONLY A RUMOUR! AT

MIDNIGHT THE SOUND-COMPETITION

BEGAN. NEARLY ALL MUSIX WERE A

KIND OF TECHNO BUT NOTHING WAS

A REAL PLEASURE FOR YOUR EARS!

THE GFX AND DEMO-COMPETITON

WERE CANCELED COZ OF A DEFECT

OF THE RGB-SCREEN-PROJECTOR!

BUT NEVER THE LESS WE SAW SOME

STUNNING DEMOS DURING THAT DAY!

PAGE -6-

AT 2AM THE DISCO WAS CLOSED AND

SOME GUYS SLEPT THAT NIGHT IN

THE DISCO. AT 6AM EARLY IN THE

MORNING THE PARTY SHOULD BE

CONTINUED THE FOLLOWING DAY!

WE ALL RETURNED THAT NIGHT HOME

AND SLEPT WELL IN OUR OWN BEDS

...
